TOP 3 MOVIES WATCHED IN TRAILER ON YOU TUBE

1. Saalar part 1 Cease Fire - 113.2 million & BOX OFFICE COLLECTION: ₹ 700 crores 🤑



Salaar Part 1 – Ceasefire is a high-intensity action drama released on 22 December 2023, directed by Prashanth Neel. The film stars Prabhas in the powerful role of Deva, a man with a mysterious past and unmatched strength. Set in a violent and politically charged world, the story unfolds amid brutal power struggles and deep-rooted rivalries. The movie belongs to the Action, Drama, and Thriller genres, delivering gripping storytelling and mass-action sequences. Originally made in Telugu, the film was dubbed into Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, and Malayalam for a wider audience. It was produced by the renowned banner Hombale Films, known for large-scale cinematic projects. The intense background score and songs were composed by Ravi Basrur, enhancing the film’s dark and powerful tone. The story concludes with a dramatic ceasefire, paving the way for an explosive sequel.


2. KGF Chapter 1- 279.2 million & BOX OFFICE COLLECTION: ₹ 250 crore 😇



KGF: Chapter 1 is an action-drama film released on 21 December 2018, directed by Prashanth Neel. The movie stars Yash in the lead role as Rocky, a fearless man driven by ambition and power. Set in the 1970s and 1980s, the story traces Rocky’s rise from poverty to dominance in the criminal underworld. The narrative revolves around the deadly gold mines of Kolar Gold Fields, controlled by ruthless oppressors. The film blends intense action with emotional depth and strong character development. Produced by Hombale Films, it marked a turning point for Kannada cinema on a pan-India level. The powerful background score by Ravi Basrur elevated the film’s impact. KGF: Chapter 1 ends with Rocky asserting his authority, setting the stage for an even grander sequel.


3. SINGAM- 1.41 billion && BOX OFFICE COLLECTION: ₹ 876 million 😍



Singam is an action-packed police drama released on 28 May 2010, directed by Hari. The film stars Suriya in the lead role as Durai Singam, an honest and fearless police officer. Set in a rural backdrop, the story highlights Singam’s strict sense of justice and integrity. His life takes a dramatic turn when he confronts a powerful and corrupt criminal network. The movie strongly emphasizes law, order, and moral values. High-energy action sequences and powerful dialogues define the film. The music, composed by Devi Sri Prasad, adds intensity to key moments. Singam became a major commercial success and established itself as one of the most iconic cop films in Tamil cinema.
